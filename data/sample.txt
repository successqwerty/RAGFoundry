                  RAGFOUNDRY ENTERPRISE HR HANDBOOK
===================================================================

SECTION 1: WORK HOURS & OVERTIME
1.1 Operating Hours: Standard operating hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST, Monday through Friday.
1.2 Core Hours: All team members must be available for synchronous communication during core hours between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM EST.
1.3 Overtime: Non-exempt employees who work more than 40 hours in a single workweek are eligible for 1.5x overtime pay. All overtime must be pre-approved in writing by a department head.

SECTION 2: PAID TIME OFF (PTO) & LEAVE POLICY
2.1 Vacation Allowance: Full-time employees receive 18 paid vacation days per calendar year, accrued monthly at 1.5 days per completed month of service.
2.2 PTO Requests: Vacation requests exceeding 3 consecutive business days must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR Portal.
2.3 Sick Leave: Employees receive 7 fully paid sick leave days per year. A doctor's note is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.
2.4 Parental Leave: Primary caregivers receive 14 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth or legal adoption of a child. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave.

SECTION 3: HYBRID & REMOTE WORK POLICY
3.1 Remote Eligibility: Full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period are eligible for hybrid work (up to 2 days per week working from home).
3.2 Work Setup Allowance: Remote-eligible employees receive a one-time $500 stipend to equip their home office with ergonomic furniture and accessories.
3.3 Internet Reimbursement: Employees working remotely receive a monthly $60 internet expense reimbursement.

SECTION 4: HEALTH BENEFITS & WELLNESS
4.1 Coverage Start Date: Comprehensive health insurance (medical, dental, and vision) coverage begins on your very first day of employment.
4.2 Premium Coverage: RAGFoundry covers 85% of employee health insurance premiums and 50% of eligible dependent premiums.
4.3 Wellness Stipend: Employees receive $75 per month for physical fitness memberships, mental health apps, or wellness subscriptions.

SECTION 5: PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT & LEARNING
5.1 Annual Budget: Each full-time employee is allocated a $1,500 annual learning budget for courses, technical certifications, industry conferences, and books.
5.2 Certification Bonuses: Employees who successfully pass pre-approved technical certification exams (e.g., AWS, GCP, Azure, PMP) receive a one-time $300 cash bonus.

SECTION 6: TRAVEL & EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
6.1 Business Travel: Travel for client meetings or conferences must be booked using corporate discount rates. Domestic flights under 4 hours must be economy class.
6.2 Meal Daily Allowance (Per Diem): Employees on business travel receive a daily meal allowance of $75 ($15 breakfast, $25 lunch, $35 dinner).
6.3 Expense Submission: Expense reports with valid receipts must be submitted within 14 days of travel completion for reimbursement processing.
